favicon.svg is the source. After editing it, regenerate the other files here,
or tabs, home screens and old browsers show different icons.

Colours are from the home theme (web/assets/themes/home.css):
monstera #2e6a4d, linen #fbfaf5, ochre #d6a13a.

1. Go to https://realfavicongenerator.net and upload favicon.svg
2. iOS: no margin, no extra background, so it shows the SVG as a full
   square and iOS rounds the corners itself
   Android / web app: keep the SVG as is (rounded square)
3. Generate, download the zip, and copy these four files here:

   favicon.ico
   apple-touch-icon.png
   web-app-manifest-192x192.png
   web-app-manifest-512x512.png

   Ignore the rest: favicon.svg here is the original, the server builds its
   own manifest (internal/handlers/handlers-push.go), and nothing uses
   favicon-96x96.png or site.webmanifest.
4. If the site's file names have changed, go by size:
   .ico, 180, 192 and 512 px.
5. Check them by eye, then reload the app (browsers cache icons hard).
